How to use:

  1. Click Choose image and pick a painting variant from your images/ folder.
  2. Click the top-left corner of Pikachu in the picture.
  3. Click the opposite (bottom-right) corner.
  4. The JSON below updates with Pikachu's box. Click Copy and paste it into paintings.js.

Tip: make the box a little bigger than Pikachu so small fingers have an easier hitbox.
