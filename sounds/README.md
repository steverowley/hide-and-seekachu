# Sounds

The game plays a short "Pika!" sound when Pikachu is found. To enable it, drop a file here named exactly:

    pika.mp3

The game looks for `sounds/pika.mp3` and will play it on a successful find. **If the file is missing, the game silently skips the sound** — it never breaks.

## Where to get one

- Free SFX sites such as [freesound.org](https://freesound.org), [pixabay.com/sound-effects](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/) — search for "pika" or "squeak".
- Or record your own with a phone voice memo and convert to MP3.

## Tips

- Keep it short (well under 1 second).
- Mono is fine.
- Test volume at the same level as the painting will be displayed — many free files are louder than they sound on a desktop.
